From browser
to battlefield.

Use a desktop browser with a keyboard and mouse. For ZeroHour, start with Windows Chrome and hardware acceleration enabled.

  1. Choose your game

    Open its launcher and enter a commander name. No account needed.

  2. Import your files

    Select a compatible ZIP, RAR or game folder. Files are checked and installed locally.

  3. Take command

    Launch a solo skirmish, or use your game’s multiplayer controls to join a friend.

Private multiplayer is experimental. Full matches across separate internet networks and Mac performance are still being verified.

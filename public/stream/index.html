Play Zero Hour through a private stream. The host runs the game; you bring your keyboard and mouse.

Connect from the same Wi-Fi or over the internet. Game files and saves stay on the host laptop.

Connect to a host

Ask your host for the link and private login. You’ll enter the password on their connection page.

Hosting from your laptop?

Start your stream, then share the invite and password privately. Your friend gets a separate game session while you keep playing locally.

In your Zero Hour Web project folder, double-click Start Zero Hour Internet Streaming.cmd with Docker Desktop running. Open the private invite file printed by the launcher.

Connection and hosting details

The laptop must stay on, plugged in, and awake. Internet invites work while its tunnel is running; restarting the tunnel creates a new address. Stop Zero Hour Streaming closes public access.

For a friend on your Wi-Fi, use Start Zero Hour Streaming.cmd and its local address. Choose Same Wi-Fi on the receiver. For a public invite, choose Internet.

Import your files once in the streamed browser on the host. The receiving device needs no game installation. Both players join the same game room to play together.

Internet starts with a low-bandwidth 720p30 preset. Choose higher quality on the receiver if both connections can keep up. Internet connections add video buffering and network delay. Playback speed depends on the host, both connections, and the receiving browser. Use a desktop browser with a keyboard and mouse.

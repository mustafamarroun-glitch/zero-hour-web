# Zero Hour Web

## Platform
Desktop web, with mobile setup guidance.

## Stack
Delegated by the supplied brief: static HTML/CSS/modules, New Shoes threaded WebAssembly, local OPFS import, a separate Node signaling service.

## Purpose
Open a dedicated Zero Hour website, choose a commander name, import compatible player-owned files once, and play skirmish or a private two-player match.

## Constraints
Username-only guest identity; no account authentication. Original installations and Online-Games remain untouched. Retail archives stay local and are excluded from source and deployment. No automatic patches, mods, or substitute data. Mac gameplay and cross-network matches require independent acceptance.

## Visual commitment
Original military direction, clear typography, restrained motion, immediate play flow, no OS desktop or unrelated games. Chrono Divide is a player-journey reference only.
